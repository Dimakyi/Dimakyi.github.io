About CaféQuick

Good coffee. Less waiting.

CaféQuick is a coffee-ordering concept designed to make everyday coffee stops simpler. It gives customers a way to explore café menus and place orders ahead of time, helping them spend less time waiting and more time enjoying their day.

Why CaféQuick?

Busy mornings leave little room for unnecessary queues. Whether someone is heading to work, attending classes or travelling across the city, getting a coffee shouldn't have to mean losing valuable time.

CaféQuick was created around a simple idea: make ordering coffee more convenient while helping local cafés serve their customers more efficiently.

How it works

  • Explore local cafés: Browse participating coffee shops and discover their menus.
  • Choose your favourites: View available drinks and select what you'd like to order.
  • Order with convenience: Add items to your cart and follow the application's ordering flow.

The goal is to create a straightforward experience from browsing to ordering, with room to grow as the platform develops.

Supporting local cafés

Independent coffee shops bring character and community to the places we live and work. CaféQuick aims to make it easier for customers to discover these businesses and, over time, help cafés manage incoming orders more conveniently.

The person behind the idea

CaféQuick was developed by Dimakatso Masake, a software development student who enjoys turning everyday frustrations into practical technology ideas.

The project reflects an interest in building useful, accessible applications that connect people with local businesses. It is also an opportunity to develop software skills through hands-on work, problem-solving and continuous improvement.

Our vision

We envision a smoother coffee-ordering experience where customers can discover cafés, choose their favourite drinks and order with greater convenience.

CaféQuick — your next coffee stop, made simpler.
